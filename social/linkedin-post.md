🚀 **Introducing Poly Vault — your personal 3D & 2D asset library with a
one-click pipeline into Unity 6.**

As a game/vfx developer, my assets live everywhere — scattered folders, messy
naming, and the eternal "I know I downloaded that texture somewhere." So I
built the tool I always wanted: **Poly Vault**, a free, open-source, desktop
asset manager that runs on your own machine.

What it does:

🎛️ **Browse anything** — point it at any folder structure; it turns it into a
searchable library with preview cards, a folder tree, and per-folder stats
(counts, sizes, asset types).

🏷️ **Organise your way** — tags, descriptions, tag-based filtering, and search.

🎮 **Unity 6 import in one click** — queue any asset from the app and the
bundled Unity Editor plugin copies it straight into your project
(`Assets/PolyVaultImports/…`). Copies only — your library is never touched.

🔒 **100% offline** — local storage, loopback-only bridge, no cloud, no
accounts, no telemetry. Your assets stay yours.

The polish is in the details: three themes (Default / Dark / Light), live
"Unity connected" indicator, flicker-free UI, resizable panels, and personal
preferences that persist.

Installers for Windows, Linux, and macOS, plus the Unity package, are available
for v0.3.0:
👉 https://github.com/xeeshanqaswar/Poly-Vault/releases

Open source, MIT licensed, and built with love. Feedback, issues, and ideas
are genuinely welcome.
